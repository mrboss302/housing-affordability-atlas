Where can I afford to live on $100,000 a year?

On a household income of $100,000, a standard 10%-down scenario supports an estimated home price up to about $309,085 — keeping housing near 28% of gross income.

The assumptions behind this estimate

  • 10% down payment
  • 6.70% interest rate, 30-year fixed
  • Property tax 1.1%/yr, insurance 0.45%/yr, PMI 0.6%/yr of the loan
  • Housing capped near 28% of gross income

At the top of that budget (~$309,085), the estimated payment is about $2,333/month ($1,795 principal & interest + $283 tax + $116 insurance + $139 PMI).
