What 'rent burden' means here

Where Zillow ZORI is absent, the Atlas uses the Census ACS median gross rent and divides its annual equivalent by the median income of all households in the state. This is a high-level rent-to-income proxy, not the official Census share of renter households paying more than 30% of their own income. Do not treat our proxy as the population's true cost-burden rate.

When numbers change

Mortgage assumptions can change independently from published research data. The source month (2026-10) is not the website build date. A monthly scheduled import checks for new Zillow research data. The Census ACS reference period (ACS 5-year 2023) changes on a different release schedule, and a failed import may leave the last valid dataset in place. Dates on this page refer to the loaded datasets, not a guarantee of current listings.

Corrections and source verification

Original sources: Zillow Research data and U.S. Census ACS. Please report an incorrect number or broken source explanation via a public project issue; avoid sharing personal financial details there. The original source terms and permitted redistribution should be verified before monetization.

About these estimates & data sources

Home values are Zillow Research ZHVI (2026-10). Rent: Census ACS median gross rent (ACS 5-year 2023); the Zillow ZORI fields are unavailable in this dataset. Household income reflects U.S. Census ACS data. Rent relative to the general household median is a proxy, not the Census rent-cost-burden statistic for renter households. All payment figures are modeled planning estimates, not appraisals, quotes, or loan offers.
