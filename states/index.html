What changes when you cross state lines?

Statewide prices give a useful first comparison, but they hide the variation between cities and neighborhoods. The Atlas helps compare the scale of housing costs, not predict the price of an individual property. For a relocation decision, check real listings, local property taxes, homeowners insurance quotes and the cost of commuting.

The state comparison tool holds your income, down payment, fixed-rate assumption and home type constant, allowing you to see whether higher home prices or a different income context changes the projected budget. For exact loan costs, use Home Payment Atlas.

About these estimates & data sources

Home values are Zillow Research ZHVI (2026-10). Rent: Census ACS median gross rent (ACS 5-year 2023); the Zillow ZORI fields are unavailable in this dataset. Household income reflects U.S. Census ACS data. Rent relative to the general household median is a proxy, not the Census rent-cost-burden statistic for renter households. All payment figures are modeled planning estimates, not appraisals, quotes, or loan offers.
