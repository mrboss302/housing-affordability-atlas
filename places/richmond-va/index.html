Frequently asked questions

How much income do you need to buy in Richmond, VA?

For a typical home around $359,807 (Zillow ZHVI, 2026-10), the estimated payment is roughly $2,716/month and the income needed is near $116,400. A starter home (~$245,882) lowers that to about $79,543. Model your own scenario on Home Payment Atlas.

Is it cheaper to rent or buy in Richmond?

Median rent runs about $1,314/month (25% of income), versus an estimated $2,716/month to own a typical home. Rent-vs-buy depends on how long you stay, your down payment, and maintenance. Compare scenarios on the Home Payment Atlas calculator.

Where do these Richmond figures come from?

Home value comes from Zillow Research ZHVI (2026-10). Rent source: U.S. Census ACS median gross rent (ACS 5-year 2023). Household income and population are from the U.S. Census Bureau (ACS 5-year 2023). Rent relative to the general median income is a proxy, not official renter cost burden.
