Frequently asked questions

What is the typical home value in Baltimore County?

Zillow Research publishes home values at city, metro, and state level but not for individual counties in the datasets used here, so a Baltimore County home value is not available yet. Income and rent below are from current data; for buying math, see Maryland (typical home ~$423,482) or use the mortgage calculator.

Is it cheaper to rent or buy in Baltimore County?

Median rent runs about $1,566/month. Rent-vs-buy depends on how long you stay, your down payment, and maintenance. Compare scenarios on the Home Payment Atlas calculator.

Where do these Baltimore County figures come from?

Rent source: U.S. Census ACS median gross rent (ACS 5-year 2023). Household income and population are from the U.S. Census Bureau (ACS 5-year 2023). Rent relative to the general median income is a proxy, not official renter cost burden. Figures are estimates, not appraisals. Zillow does not endorse this site.

About these estimates & data sources

Home values are Zillow Research ZHVI (2026-10). Rent: Census ACS median gross rent (ACS 5-year 2023); the Zillow ZORI fields are unavailable in this dataset. Household income reflects U.S. Census ACS data. Rent relative to the general household median is a proxy, not the Census rent-cost-burden statistic for renter households.
