How these estimates work

Each state combines a typical home value with editable assumptions — a 6.70% rate, 30-year term, 10% down, plus property tax, insurance, and PMI — to estimate a monthly payment, then the income needed so that payment is about 28% of gross pay. Read the full methodology.

About these estimates & data sources

Home values are Zillow Research ZHVI (2026-10). Rent: Census ACS median gross rent (ACS 5-year 2023); the Zillow ZORI fields are unavailable in this dataset. Household income reflects U.S. Census ACS data. Rent relative to the general household median is a proxy, not the Census rent-cost-burden statistic for renter households. All payment figures are modeled planning estimates, not appraisals, quotes, or loan offers.

Home values: Zillow Research ZHVI (2026-10). Rent: U.S. Census ACS 5-year median gross rent (ACS 5-year 2023). The rent-to-income figure is a proxy, not a Census renter-cost-burden statistic.
