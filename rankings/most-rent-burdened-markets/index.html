How this ranking is built

The ratio divides median gross rent by overall median household income, annualized. It is not the official Census renter cost-burden statistic; renters may have very different incomes from the overall median. Every figure is an educational estimate. See the full methodology, then customize the math on the Home Payment Atlas mortgage calculator.

About these estimates & data sources

Home values are Zillow Research ZHVI (2026-10). Rent: Census ACS median gross rent (ACS 5-year 2023); the Zillow ZORI fields are unavailable in this dataset. Household income reflects U.S. Census ACS data. Rent relative to the general household median is a proxy, not the Census rent-cost-burden statistic for renter households.
