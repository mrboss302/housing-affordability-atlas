Which housing ranking should you use?

Home values describe Zillow's market tier estimates, not current listing prices. Buying affordability compares an illustrative mortgage payment to an income benchmark. Income needed is a modeled payment divided by a housing-income guideline. These answer different questions and should not be treated as a single universal ranking.

For a specific income and two possible destinations, use Compare States. To choose a state alphabetically, open the state directory. Review methodology and source dates before making a financial decision.

About these estimates & data sources

Home values are Zillow Research ZHVI (2026-10). Rent: Census ACS median gross rent (ACS 5-year 2023); the Zillow ZORI fields are unavailable in this dataset. Household income reflects U.S. Census ACS data. Rent relative to the general household median is a proxy, not the Census rent-cost-burden statistic for renter households. All payment figures are modeled planning estimates, not appraisals, quotes, or loan offers.
