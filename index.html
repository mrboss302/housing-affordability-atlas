A market-discovery map, not a listings site

Most housing sites show you individual listings. This one shows you the landscape: which states and markets are within reach for a given income, where rent eats the most of a paycheck, and where starter homes are still attainable. Home values come from Zillow Research (ZHVI), while rent is taken from Census ACS median gross rent (ACS 5-year 2023); the Zillow ZORI fields are unavailable in this dataset. Household income and geography come from the U.S. Census Bureau. When you find a market worth a closer look, continue to Home Payment Atlas to customize down payment, taxes, PMI, insurance, and HOA.

About these estimates & data sources

Home values are Zillow Research ZHVI (2026-10). Rent: Census ACS median gross rent (ACS 5-year 2023); the Zillow ZORI fields are unavailable in this dataset. Household income reflects U.S. Census ACS data. Rent relative to the general household median is a proxy, not the Census rent-cost-burden statistic for renter households. All payment figures are modeled planning estimates, not appraisals, quotes, or loan offers.

Home values: Zillow Research ZHVI (2026-10). Rent: U.S. Census ACS 5-year median gross rent (ACS 5-year 2023). The rent-to-income figure is a proxy, not a Census renter-cost-burden statistic. Geography and FIPS codes: U.S. Census Bureau (Cartographic Boundary Files). Household income: U.S. Census ACS (ACS 5-year 2023). Detailed payment scenarios: Home Payment Atlas. Zillow does not endorse this site.

Figures are educational estimates only and are not financial, legal, tax, mortgage, or real-estate advice, and not a loan offer. Verify every number with lenders, agents, tax authorities, and official sources. See our methodology and terms & disclaimer.
