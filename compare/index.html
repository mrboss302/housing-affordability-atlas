How to read this comparison

The same financing assumptions

To isolate the price differences in statewide housing markets, both cards use the same hypothetical fixed rate and down payment. Actual quoted rates depend on borrower credit, the loan program, lenders, and property-specific factors. The one-point stress figure recomputes principal and interest without changing other assumptions.

Income gap, not mortgage approval

The monthly budget gap compares the estimated payment with 28% of your gross monthly household income. A negative value means the modeled payment is above that guideline. It does not establish whether a bank will approve a mortgage, and it excludes non-housing debts and household expenses.

One reproducible example

A hypothetical $400,000 home at 10% down, a 6.7% fixed mortgage rate over 30 years, 1.10% annual property tax, 0.45% annual insurance and modeled PMI produces about $3,020 per month. This corresponds to about $129,429 annual gross income at the 28% housing guideline. You can reproduce it by entering the numbers in Home Payment Atlas; these are not current lender terms.

Why area averages are not listings

Zillow's home-value index measures typical values within property-market tiers, not current asking prices or appraisals. Census median gross rent covers different households and property types, so the rent-to-income ratio is only a broad proxy. For a decision, compare exact addresses, local property tax assessments, insurance quotes, lenders' offers and travel costs.

About these estimates & data sources

Home values are Zillow Research ZHVI (2026-10). Rent: Census ACS median gross rent (ACS 5-year 2023); the Zillow ZORI fields are unavailable in this dataset. Household income reflects U.S. Census ACS data. Rent relative to the general household median is a proxy, not the Census rent-cost-burden statistic for renter households. All payment figures are modeled planning estimates, not appraisals, quotes, or loan offers.
